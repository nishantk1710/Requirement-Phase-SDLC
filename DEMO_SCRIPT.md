# RGA — Demo Video Scripts

*One document, two versions: the **full ~5‑minute** walkthrough and a tighter **~4‑minute** version. Same structure, same on‑screen cues — pick whichever fits your slot. Language is natural and conversational, but still boardroom‑appropriate.*

> **Before you hit record:** run a project once beforehand so everything's cached and the stages fly by. Start on the Input tab. And a quick heads‑up — **don't open the `.env` file or settings on screen; it holds a private key.**

---
---

# VERSION A — Full walkthrough (≈ 5 min)

## 0 · Opening (≈ 30 sec)
**[ON SCREEN: the home page.]**

> "Every software project kicks off the same way — with a mountain of documents. Business requirements, meeting notes, email chains, backlogs. And somebody has to turn all of that into one clear, agreed‑upon spec. It's slow, it's manual, and honestly, it's really easy to miss things.
>
> That's the problem we built **RGA** to solve. Over the next few minutes, I'll show you how it takes those raw, messy documents and turns them into a clean, review‑ready requirements document — with a person signing off on every step along the way."

**[Segue:]** "Let me show you how it works, from start to finish."

## 1 · Input — what goes in (≈ 50 sec)
**[ON SCREEN: click the Input tab; show the dropdown, then the file table.]**

> "It all starts here. We pick a set of project documents — or we can upload our own.
>
> And the moment we choose a set, the system shows us exactly what it's about to read. Every file, its format, its size. So there are no surprises — we always know precisely what went in."

**[ON SCREEN: gesture to the six files.]**

> "Here we've got six documents — a requirements doc, a discovery call, an email thread, some notes, an intake form, and a product backlog. A pretty typical starting point.
>
> Now we just click **Run**."

**[ON SCREEN: click "Run the agent pipeline".]**

**[Segue:]** "And this is where it gets clever."

## 2 · Run — the agents at work (≈ 55 sec)
**[ON SCREEN: the stage tracker, steps ticking green.]**

> "Behind the scenes, a team of AI agents reads through everything the way a seasoned analyst would — except in seconds, not days.
>
> They pull out each requirement, clear out the duplicates, merge everything into one tidy list, check what's in and out of scope, flag anything that contradicts, and even run a second opinion to catch the shaky ones."

**[ON SCREEN: steps completing with ticks.]**

> "Every step ticks off as it finishes, so you can follow along — it's not a black box. And here's the key part: none of this is final. **A human still approves everything.**"

**[Segue:]** "Which takes us to the screen that matters most — the review."

## 3 · Review — the human decides (≈ 90 sec)
**[ON SCREEN: the "Review by decision" page.]**

> "Now, we could ask a reviewer to read hundreds of requirements one at a time — but nobody enjoys that. So instead, RGA boils it down to a short list of real **decisions**. And for each one, it tells you what it recommends, and why."

**[ON SCREEN: open a decision card — the conflict.]**

> "Take this one. It's spotted two statements that can't both be true. It lays them out in plain English, recommends the stronger option — but the final call is ours. We accept it, or we overrule it. And whatever we decide gets applied everywhere it matters, automatically."

**[ON SCREEN: scroll to Technology Stack; show the recommended pick and the "Other" field.]**

> "It'll also suggest a sensible technology stack — the tools you'd actually build this with — with a recommended choice for each area. Happy with it? Leave it. Prefer your own? Just type it in."

**[ON SCREEN: scroll to the bottom; the "Apply all recommended" and "Generate" buttons.]**

> "Once we've been through it all, we apply the recommendations in a single click — and then we generate the documents."

**[ON SCREEN: click "Generate SRS / RTM".]**

**[Segue:]** "Give it a few seconds… and here's the payoff."

## 4 · Output — the finished spec (≈ 55 sec)
**[ON SCREEN: the Generated documents tab, SRS preview.]**

> "This is a complete **Software Requirements Specification** — a proper, industry‑standard document that would normally take a team the better part of a week to write. Scope, every approved requirement, the technology stack, all of it."

**[ON SCREEN: switch to the Traceability Matrix tab.]**

> "And right alongside it, a **traceability matrix**. This is the part I love — it links every single requirement back to the exact line in the original documents it came from. So there's a clear, auditable trail. Nothing's made up, and nothing falls through the cracks."

**[ON SCREEN: click "Download (.docx)".]**

> "And it's ready to hand over. One click, and it downloads as a Word document — formatted to flow straight into the design phase."

**[Segue:]** "So let me pull it all together."

## 5 · Closing (≈ 25 sec)
**[ON SCREEN: back to the finished SRS or the home page.]**

> "In just a few minutes, RGA took a stack of messy documents and turned them into a clean, approved, fully traceable specification — with a person in control the whole way through.
>
> What used to eat up days now takes an afternoon — with fewer mistakes, and complete clarity from start to finish. Thanks for watching."

### Delivery notes (both versions)
- **Slow down** — the visuals carry a lot; a calm pace always sounds more confident than a rushed one.
- **Define two terms once, casually:** *SRS* → "the requirements document," *traceability* → "the trail back to the source."
- **Version B trims** the pipeline detail and merges Input + Run — reach for it if your slot is tight or the audience is very non‑technical.
