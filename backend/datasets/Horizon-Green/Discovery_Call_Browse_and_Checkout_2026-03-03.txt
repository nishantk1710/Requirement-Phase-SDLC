Project Horizon — Discovery Call: Browse, Search & Checkout
Date: 3 March 2026, 11:00 IST
Attendees: Karan Malhotra (Product), Ananya Bose (Growth), Dev Prasad (Engineering), facilitator (CommerceForge)

Facilitator: Let's cover how people find and buy things. Karan, what's the core?

Karan: Browsing and finding products. On the product listing page people want to combine filters —
brand, price and rating at once — and see how many results each option has. And search needs
autocomplete as you type, with some tolerance for small spelling mistakes.

Karan: On the product page, each size/colour is its own SKU with its own stock, so the variant picker
resolves to a specific SKU and add-to-cart adds that SKU. Show the price inclusive of tax on the page.

Facilitator: Cart and checkout?

Karan: The cart should persist across sessions for a logged-in customer. Checkout should be short:
address, delivery method, a review with the totals and taxes, then payment, then confirmation.

Ananya: The one I'll push for is guest checkout — let people check out with just email and address,
and offer to create an account afterwards. The BRD still lists this as "open" — Meera hasn't signed off.

Karan: Noted as open; we'll resolve it.

Facilitator: Good — that's browse, search, product detail, cart and the checkout shape. Payments and tax
are a separate thread with Finance and the vendor.
