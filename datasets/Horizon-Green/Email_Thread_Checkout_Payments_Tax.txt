Karan — clean way to scope it:

- We build a provider-agnostic payment module behind one interface, with a built-in test-mode mock that
  simulates success, failure and pending. So the whole checkout and order flow is fully built and
  testable now, with no live keys. Going live is a configuration change, not a code change.
- Flow rule: an order is NOT confirmed until the payment callback confirms success. If payment fails,
  times out or is pending, the customer keeps their cart and can retry.

Finance side:

- Every order must generate a GST tax invoice as a downloadable PDF, with the correct tax split.
- The price shown to the customer is tax-inclusive, but the invoice must still show the tax breakup.
- A refund must be recorded against the order.

On returns, the window should be 7 days from a books point of view — though CX is pushing for 10. Let's
settle 7 vs 10 before it goes into the spec.
