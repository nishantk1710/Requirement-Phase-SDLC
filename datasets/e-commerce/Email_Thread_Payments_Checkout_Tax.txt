Karan,

On the tech side:

- We'll integrate a PSP's hosted checkout (redirect or embedded), so card details are
  entered on the provider's side and tokenised there. Card numbers never hit our servers or
  DB — that keeps PCI-DSS scope with the provider, which is what you want. We only keep a
  token / transaction reference and the payment status against the order.
- Recommend enabling saved cards for returning customers, but via the provider's tokenisation,
  not us storing anything. Speeds up repeat checkout a lot.
- Methods are mostly a config/enablement question with the provider — cards, UPI, netbanking,
  wallets are all standard. COD is different, that's not a gateway thing, that's an operational
  flow (collect cash, reconcile, handle non-delivery) and it needs Ops to own it.
- Suggestion: keep checkout as few steps as possible. Address -> delivery -> pay. An express
  path for logged-in users with a saved address + saved card is worth doing.

One thing I need back: partial refunds. If someone returns one item out of a three-item order,
do you want to refund just that line, or is it whole-order only? Affects how we structure it.

Hi both,

Finance/compliance side — this is not optional:

- Every order must produce a proper GST tax invoice. That means our GSTIN, the customer's
  details, HSN/SAC codes per line item, and the correct tax breakup (CGST+SGST for intra-state,
  IGST for inter-state). It has to be downloadable as a PDF and also emailed to the customer.
- The price shown to the customer should be inclusive of tax (that's the norm here), but the
  invoice must still show the split.
- For refunds we need a credit note generated, not just a silent reversal, for our books.

Partial refunds — yes, we absolutely need line-level / partial refunds. Whole-order-only will
not work, people return single items all the time.

On COD I'll defer to Ops but from a finance view COD is a reconciliation headache and has
higher return-to-origin rates, so if we do it I'd want it capped (e.g. not on high-value orders).

Priyanka
