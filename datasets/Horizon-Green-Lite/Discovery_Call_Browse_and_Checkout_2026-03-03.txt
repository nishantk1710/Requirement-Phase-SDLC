Project Horizon — Discovery Call: Browse & Checkout
Date: 15 July 2026, 11:00 IST
Attendees: Karan Malhotra (Product), Ananya Bose (Growth), CommerceForge

Karan: Browsing is the main gap. On the listing page people should be able to filter by category, brand
and price together, sort the results, and page through large categories. It has to work well on mobile.

Ananya: And search needs autocomplete as you type. On the product page, each size/colour is its own SKU
with its own stock, so the variant picker must resolve to a specific SKU before add-to-cart. Show the
price with the discount, tax-inclusive.

Karan: Cart should persist across devices for logged-in users. Checkout should feel fast — keep it to
address, delivery, review, pay, confirm. People should be able to enter a promo code and see the discount
before paying.

Ananya: And please allow guest checkout — don't force account creation to buy. It's still marked "open"
in the BRD but it matters for conversion.

Karan: Noted, Meera hasn't signed that off yet. That's browse, search, product page, cart and checkout —
payments and tax are a separate thread with Finance.
