Subject: RE: Payments & tax — how we build it

Clean approach: we build a provider-agnostic payment module behind one interface (authorize, capture,
refund, get-status) with a built-in test-mode mock that simulates success/failure/pending and fires the
confirmation callback internally. The whole checkout and order flow is built and testable now with no
live keys. At go-live you add the real provider's keys via configuration; core code doesn't change. Card
data never hits our servers. Rule: an order is not confirmed until the payment callback confirms success,
and a failed payment keeps the cart so the customer can retry.

Tarun

Finance: every order needs a GST tax-invoice PDF with our GSTIN, the customer's details and the correct
split (CGST+SGST intra-state, IGST inter-state). Price shown is tax-inclusive but the invoice must show
the breakup. Refunds must generate a credit note and support partial (line-level) refunds. We do NOT
need government e-invoicing for launch. On returns, the window should be 7 days.

Sameer (CX view): 7 days is tight — competitors give 10. I'd push for a 10-day window. Let's settle this
before it goes in the spec.
